ℹ️ How to Use Move-It

What is Move-It?

Move-It is a smart activity blocker that helps you stay active during your workday. When your focus timer runs out, your screen locks, and you have to complete a quick stretch or dance to unlock it!

The Focus Timer

Set how long you want to focus before taking a break. Click START TRACKING to begin the countdown.

When Time's Up

When the timer hits zero, the Move-It prompt appears. You can choose to:

  • DANCE: Follow along with a short dance video. The camera tracks your moves and gives you a score!
  • STRETCH: Follow along with stretching poses. Hold each pose until the timer fills up.
  • SKIP: If you really can't move right now, you can skip. But beware: your next focus session limit will be halved as a penalty (down to a minimum of 10 minutes)!

Meeting Mode

If you're in a meeting and don't want to be interrupted, toggle MEETING MODE ON. The timer will pause until you turn it off.

The Green Tray Icon

When you close the main window (by clicking the red 'X' in the top right corner) or minimize it, Move-It keeps running in the background. Look for the little green Move-It icon in your system tray (bottom right corner of your screen, near the clock). You can right-click it for quick options like showing the app again, pausing tracking, or completely exiting the app.
